Why capital flow

Price is only half the story.

Most indicators only look at the chart in front of you. Crypto doesn't trade on its own, though. A rising dollar, a volatility spike or money rotating out of alts can all undo a clean-looking setup.

01

Momentum without context

Oscillators tell you a move is stretched. They can't tell you whether the money behind it is still arriving or already leaving.

02

Too many tabs

Checking DXY, VIX, yields, BTC.D and TOTAL3 by hand before every trade is slow, and it's easy to skip when the market moves fast.

03

Signals that move after the fact

Lots of multi-timeframe tools read unfinished higher-timeframe bars, so their history looks better than it was live. The suite only uses closed bars.

How it works

Eight inputs in, one score out.

Each input has a preset direction for the market you're trading. For crypto, a rising dollar or VIX counts against you. The engine weights the inputs, checks how many agree, and gives you a single normalised score.

−100%−500+50+100%
> 70%Full Send. The strongest agreement between inputs.
> 50%Standard. A normal directional signal, and the entry the backtest uses.
> 20%Lean. An early tilt. Treat it as a heads-up.
±20%Neutral. Mixed flows. Usually a good time to wait.

Before it flips direction, the score has to clear ±20% for two bars. That cuts down the whipsaw you get from a simple zero-cross.

Reading the oscillator

Timing from the waves, permission from the flow.

The waves show momentum. The band along the bottom shows whether capital agrees. A turn only grades A or B when the wave was stretched and most of the checks line up.

  • Momentum waves. WaveTrend-style, or a "Cloud Position" view inside the Keltner cloud.
  • Higher-timeframe wave. A soft lavender line: 1H waves on 15m charts, 4H on 1H, daily on 4H. Closed bars only.
  • Money flow. Buy vs sell volume from lower-timeframe candles, smoothed so it reads as a curve, not a sawtooth.
  • Yellow momentum area. A second, smoother read of momentum.
  • Capital-flow band. The same engine and presets as the overlay. It only changes shade when the reading holds for 3 bars.
  • Graded dots. A = gold or pink, B = outlined dot, C = small dot. Small by design, so they don't bury the waves.
  • Divergence and RSI cue. Divergence lines (bold when wave, RSI and money flow all agree), and a wave edge that changes colour at RSI extremes.
Trading modes

It adjusts to your timeframe.

The mode is picked from your chart's timeframe, so the data it reads matches how long you plan to hold.

Scalp

1m–15m charts · 5m/15m/1H data

Macro becomes a lighter 1H/4H/1D backdrop. On crypto, the leader and sector slots switch to order flow: open interest × price, Coinbase premium and perp basis. Thresholds adapt to volatility.

Intraday

30m–4H charts · 15m/1H/4H data

The full 8-input basket on fixed thresholds. This is the range the published backtests cover.

Swing

Daily+ charts · 4H/1D/1W data

A slower backdrop for your overall bias. It's for deciding which side to trade, not for frequent entries.

Read this before the numbers

  • Small sample. Only 15–20 trades per timeframe. A few trades going the other way would change the picture a lot.
  • Chosen after the fact. We picked this setup after trying about 10 configurations, so expect it to look better here than it will going forward.
  • Mostly a bull market. A long-only system had the wind behind it. We haven't validated it on a bear market such as 2022, or on ETH.
  • 3H and 4H are fragile. Those results depend on an unintended interaction in how lower-timeframe data gets scaled inside the script. Every cleaner rewrite we tried made much less (3H fell from +47% to about +19%). Treat the 3H and 4H rows as unproven.
  • Shorts lost money on every timeframe. Trading both directions on every flip did worse than buy & hold.
  • Past results don't predict future results. This is a filter to help you decide, not a system to follow blindly.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. Both indicators are free, and there's no paid tier, trial or upsell for them. The site does contain affiliate links to MDX ALGO. That's how the project is supported, and it's disclosed in the footer.

Do I need MDX ALGO or any other paid indicator?

No. Version 4.6 is standalone and fetches all of its own data. An older version (v4.4) read from paid panes. v4.6 replaces it.

Does it repaint?

No. Higher-timeframe data is read from closed bars only, so a signal you see in the history is the same one you would have seen live once the bar closed.

Does it work outside crypto?

Yes. The Auto market preset switches the input basket for equities, metals and forex. Crypto is where it has been tested most, and the published backtests are BTC only.

Should I trade shorts with it?

In our backtests, shorts lost money on every timeframe we tested. The strategy default is long-only. A bearish reading can still be useful as a reason to stand aside or reduce risk.

Can I rely on the backtest numbers?

Treat them with caution. There were only 15–20 trades per timeframe, the setup was picked after testing several, the period was mostly bullish, and the 3H/4H results rest on a fragile quirk in the code. No indicator is right all the time, and you can lose money using this one.

Is this financial advice?

No. Everything here is educational. Please read the full disclaimer before using any signal.
